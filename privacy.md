# Privacy Policy for Masq AI

**Last updated: September 7, 2026**

Masq AI ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Masq AI.

## 1. How Masq AI Works

Masq AI allows users to upload or select images and use artificial intelligence to create costume, makeup, and other visual transformations.

## 2. Photos and AI Processing

When you choose a photo for an AI transformation, the image is processed as necessary to provide the requested AI generation.

Photos are used for the purpose of providing the requested functionality and are not sold to third parties.

Please only upload photos that you have the right to use.

## 3. Information We May Process

Depending on how you use the App, we may process limited information necessary to operate Masq AI, including:

- Images you choose to submit for AI processing
- App usage and technical information
- Purchase and credit/Looks information
- Information necessary to detect errors and maintain the App

## 4. Purchases and Looks

Masq AI offers consumable in-app purchases called "Looks."

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not directly receive or store your payment card information.

RevenueCat is used to help manage in-app purchases and purchased Looks. RevenueCat may process purchase-related identifiers and transaction information necessary to provide these services.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Masq AI may use third-party service providers to provide features such as AI image processing, in-app purchases, infrastructure, and app functionality.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and according to their own privacy practices.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the security of the service.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Masq AI is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
